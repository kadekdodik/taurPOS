## Keep Taking Orders, No Matter What

Unlike cloud-only systems that freeze the moment your connection buffers, *taurPOS* runs smoothly over your local venue network. Your POS terminals, kitchen displays, and receipt printers communicate instantly, keeping your lines moving and your tables turning without frustrating delays.

## Everything Your Hospitality Venue Needs

* **Effortless Table & Order Management:** Handle dine-in, takeaway, split bills, merged tables, and custom item modifiers with a clean, touch-ready interface.
* **Instant Kitchen Coordination:** Route kitchen tickets directly to station-scoped Kitchen Display Systems (KDS) or thermal order printers to eliminate communication errors.
* **Smart Inventory & Stock Control:** Track ingredient adjustments, manage purchase orders, and record goods received so you never run out of your best-sellers.
* **Hardware & Printing Flexibility:** Print customer receipts, bills, and station tickets across network and thermal receipt printers with zero hassle.
* **Clear Reports & Staff Roles:** Keep cash flows transparent with built-in audit logs, sales history, and custom permissions for managers, cashiers, waitstaff, and kitchen teams.
